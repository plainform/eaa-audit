# eaa-audit Pro — $49, one time

The free version tells you what is wrong. Pro closes the loop: it fixes the code
and produces the documents an auditor asks for.

**Status: not shipped yet.** Honest about that below.

---

## What is included

### 1. Framework-aware remediation
The free audit gives you `file:line`. Pro walks them with Claude and applies the
fix in the idiom of your stack — React, Vue, Svelte or plain HTML — then re-runs
the detector so you watch the blocking count go to zero.

### 2. Draft accessibility statement
Generated from your **actual findings**, not a template with blanks. It states
what was tested, what was found, what was fixed, and what remains outstanding
with a target date.

A draft, and it says so. It is the starting point for a real one, which a human
has to own.

### 3. CI regression guard
Conformance decays on every deploy. A check that fails the build when a new
blocking violation appears, with a baseline so an existing backlog does not stop
you shipping on day one.

### 4. Auditor mapping
Every finding laid out by **EN 301 549 clause**, the way an auditor or a law
firm reads it, not the way a developer does. Exportable.

---

## What it still will not do

The same limit as the free version, and it does not move: **automated testing
reaches 30-40% of the WCAG criteria.**

Pro will not tell you that you are compliant. It fixes what is provably wrong in
your source, documents it, and keeps it fixed. The rest — screen reader testing,
meaningful sequence, whether your alt text is actually *good* — needs a human,
and Pro tells you which criteria those are.

No legal advice. For a legal notice, talk to a professional.
